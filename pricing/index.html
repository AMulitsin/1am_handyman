Questions

About Our Pricing

Are the prices on this page guaranteed?
+
No. All prices shown are starting estimates based on typical projects under standard conditions. Every home is different. Final pricing is always confirmed after a free on-site evaluation before any work begins.
What's the minimum charge?
+
$120 for any service call. This covers the first hour of work and travel to your location. Even the smallest jobs have this minimum.
Is there a discount for multiple items?
+
Yes. When we do the same service more than once in a single visit — for example, replacing 3 outlets or hanging 4 pictures — each additional unit gets 10% off, up to a maximum of 40% discount.
Is the estimate visit really free?
+
Yes. We come out, assess the job, give you a clear price. No charge for the visit, no pressure to book.

1AM Handyman is not a licensed contractor. We provide small handyman repairs and home maintenance services within California handyman exemption limits. Work requiring a California Contractor's License is referred to licensed professionals. All prices are starting estimates — final pricing confirmed after free on-site evaluation.
